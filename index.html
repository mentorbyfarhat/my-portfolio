About Me

My Journey

My journey has been shaped by the love and sacrifices of my parents and the guidance of mentors who have made a lasting difference in my life. Their wisdom and encouragement have influenced my path in meaningful ways.

I am equally grateful to the many teachers, leaders, colleagues, friends, and communities who have challenged, supported, and inspired me along the way.

Every experience, connection, and lesson has shaped who I am today. I love working with and supporting upcoming professionals, helping them discover their potential, build confidence, and navigate their journey.

As I continue my journey across AI, technology, entrepreneurship, mentoring, and community building, I remain committed to learning, giving back, and creating meaningful impact.
